Every crypto trade here settles on a screenshot.

A screenshot of a bank app takes about forty seconds to forge. Alert replaces it with the bank's own cryptographic signature, which nobody can forge and which the bank never had to agree to. The amount is proven. Your name, account number and balance are not.

What a proof establishes

Your bank states that this amount left your account toward this destination. Nigerian transfers over NIBSS are near instant, so that is almost always the same as arrival, but a transfer can debit and later reverse. This replaces a forgeable screenshot with a bank-signed attestation. It is not a claim of final settlement.

Limits worth knowing

One bank today, Moniepoint, because each bank's alert format needs its own circuit. The buyer must pay from a bank that emails debit alerts: Moniepoint does, OPay does not. The signing key for moniepoint.com is held by their mail provider, so the attestation is "signed by Moniepoint's mail provider" rather than by Moniepoint directly.

Custody

No owner, no admin, no pause and no upgrade path exists on the escrow. USDC leaves on a valid proof, to the buyer, or at the deadline, back to the seller. Nobody else can move it, including whoever deployed it.
