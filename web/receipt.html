What the proof reveals

  • The amount, exactly as the bank printed it
  • That Moniepoint signed the email
  • A one-time reference, so it cannot be reused
  • The last five digits of the destination

What stays private

  • The account holder's name
  • The full account number
  • The account balance
  • The counterparty's name
  • Every other line of the email

A real Moniepoint alert prints the account number unmasked, next to the balance. Settling a ₦160,000 trade should not publish either, so it does not.

What this proves, precisely. The buyer's bank states that this amount left their account toward this destination. Nigerian transfers over NIBSS are near instant, so that is almost always the same as arrival, but a transfer can debit and later reverse. This replaces a forgeable screenshot with a bank-signed attestation. It is not a claim of final settlement.
